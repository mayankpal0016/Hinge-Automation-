Could my Hinge account get banned?

Yes, it's possible. Hinge, Tinder and Bumble don't permit third-party automation, and accounts using it can be restricted or banned. We can't promise otherwise. If that risk isn't acceptable to you, please don't sign up.

What does it cost?

Getting set up with Dating Auto Pilot is a one-time ₹399, for lifetime. If you also choose to let Pilot set up a dating app's paid plan, such as Hinge's, that plan is billed to you by the app, only after you approve it, and you can cancel it any time in the app or your app store settings.

Will it message people as me?

It starts conversations in the tone you pick, using only true details from your own profile. Once you're chatting with someone, the conversation is yours.

How many matches will I get?

We don't promise a number. It depends on your profile, your city and how specific your preferences are. What Pilot gives you is consistency and speed, so you stop missing the matches you do get.

Can I stop any time?

Yes. Pause or stop Pilot with one tap, and ask us to delete your data whenever you like.
